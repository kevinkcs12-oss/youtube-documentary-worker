# Pilot 01 — v1.2g Human Review Kit QA and Red-Team

Status: **validated review kit; no subjective promotion decision made**.

## Deliverables

- A full 1280×720 captioned v1.2g review proxy for continuous viewing.
- An exact 89.000-second, 2,225-frame side-by-side v1.2f/v1.2g comparison covering all five motion interventions.
- Two contact sheets and a six-row editorial scorecard.
- A deterministic builder that recreates both videos from the frozen v1.2f baseline, v1.2g candidate, and canonical SRT.

## Technical QA

- Full-film proxy: 597.760 seconds, 14,944 frames, 1280×720, 25 fps, mono 48 kHz audio; full decode passed.
- Comparison reel: 89.000 seconds, 2,225 frames, 1920×600, 25 fps, mono 48 kHz audio; full decode passed.
- Comparison windows include two seconds of context around each intervention where available.
- The 161-cue canonical SRT is used unchanged. Independent parsing confirms zero cues above two text lines.
- Final captions remain inside the lower player/caption region. They may cover tertiary lower labels by design, while upper-band evidence source and scope labels remain visible.

## Red-team history

Three intermediate states were rejected:

1. The first comparison graph opened ten subtitle renderers concurrently and was terminated by the runtime memory limit.
2. The first sequential concat completed but emitted non-monotonic audio timestamp corrections and produced 89.045 seconds / 2,226 frames.
3. The first caption-proxy styles were too large for two-line cues and encroached on the evidence-reading area.

The retained builder renders comparison windows sequentially, concatenates them through a normalized audio/video filter, hard-gates the reel to 89.000 seconds / 2,225 frames, and uses a smaller lower-band caption style.

## Editorial decision boundary

This kit makes the remaining human decision efficient; it does not substitute for it. Retain v1.2g only if continuous viewing and the five comparison windows show a clearer reading order without making the film feel like a tutorial or suggesting measurement. Otherwise revert the outline pass and keep v1.2f unchanged.

The blind voice audition remains a separate human listening gate. No voice inference is made from file metrics.
